Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Moto Studio is a private desktop tool used only by its owner (TikTok @omaryehiaax) on his own computer.

What data it reads

With the owner's permission through Login with TikTok, it reads the owner's own basic profile, account statistics (followers, likes, video count) and the list of his own videos with their public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares).

How the data is used

The data is used only to analyse the owner's own content performance. It is stored only on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared, published or sent to any third party.

What it does not do

It does not post, upload, comment, follow or like anything, and it does not access any other user's data.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services) and delete the local files.

Contact

Contact the owner through TikTok: @omaryehiaax.
